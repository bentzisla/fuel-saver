## User-reported issues

### 1. Hundreds or thousands of L/100km
**Root cause, confirmed from the user's phone DB.** The vehicle profile stored
`engineDisplacementL = 1800` (cc typed into a liters field). On the 2008 Civic, MAF (PID 10) replies
are intermittent (for example 2 of 282 samples in one session). When MAF is missing, fuel rate falls
back to speed-density (MAP × RPM × displacement), which came out 1000× too high: about 2,000–4,000 L/h.
One 4.4 km trip logged 345.8 L, and the learned bins showed 150–9,000 L/100km.
A second, smaller cause: live "instant" L/100km was fuel ÷ speed per sample. That reads
750–1,000 L/100km when pulling away at 2 km/h.

**Fixes.**
- Displacement given in cc is converted to liters, and values outside 0.6–8.0 L are treated as unknown.
- Fuel rates are capped at a plausible maximum.
- Missing MAF readings no longer switch the estimator silently.
- Live L/100km is smoothed over distance and time; below crawl speed the app shows L/h.
- The reply parser is stricter.
- At first start, a background repair (`LearnedDataRepair`) rebuilds the learned bins and affected
  trips from the retained raw samples. It writes a JSON archive (`files/repairs/`) before changing
  anything.

### 2. OBD connects only after physically replugging the dongle
**Evidence.** The last persisted error was `INIT TIMEOUT` on a `00:00:00:33:33:33` ELM327 v1.5 clone.

**Code-level causes found.**
- The socket read timeout never worked (`mSocket` is a `LocalSocket`).
- The init timeouts could not fire, so slow replies and dead links were both reported as `INIT TIMEOUT`.
- Sockets leaked on stop, on cancel and on overlapping starts.
- The ACL receiver reacted to our own reconnect drops.

These leave a single-connection clone holding a stale link, which only a power cycle clears. This is
the most likely explanation but hasn't been confirmed on the car.

**Fixes.**
- `ElmLink` enforces real per-command deadlines by closing the socket when one passes.
- Connects are single-flight per dongle address, with a cool-down of at least 1 s after a close.
- Init sends a bare `\r` first, then tries `ATZ` twice, then `ATWS`, then `ATD`, and reopens once on
  failure.
- `ATPC` is sent on every close.
- New error codes tell a timeout apart from an EOF, a write failure and a read error.
- Every connect and init step is logged verbosely.

### 3. Crowded UI / poor UX
Redesigned around one primary action per screen, glanceable numbers and progressive disclosure:
- Tabs: מסלול / נסיעה / רכב / הגדרות.
- The route screen shows the recommended route and its saving, with a one-tap navigate button.
- The live-drive screen leads with the connection state.
See the UI doc for the complete relocation table.

## Code-review fixes
- A silent dongle could freeze the UI thread (`runBlocking` stop) — fixed (obd-link).
- A learned-curve reset, adopt or backup import was overwritten by the in-memory bins while logging
  — fixed with additive `SpeedBinDao.addDeltas` (obd-data).
- History savings ignored the selected route — fixed (app-fixes).
- Overlapping route searches raced — fixed (app-fixes).
