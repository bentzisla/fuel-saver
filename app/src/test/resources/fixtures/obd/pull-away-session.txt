# Pull-away from a traffic light, recorded shape of a cheap ELM327 clone (headers off, spaces on).
# Each loop polls, in order: 010D speed, 010C rpm, 0105 coolant, 0110 MAF, 015E fuel rate.
# "delay=NN" before a loop's first command = loop period in ms (used as virtual time by the test).
# Contains the real-world pathologies behind the "hundreds / thousands of L/100 km" report:
#  - 2-3 km/h at 15-20 L/h while pulling away (old per-sample fuelRate/speed*100 = 500-1000 L/100)
#  - speed sentinel 0xFF (255 km/h), MAF sentinel FF FF (655.35 g/s)
#  - a stale reply of a different PID arriving late (desync), a truncated reply without prompt
#  - a corrupt frame flagged "<DATA ERROR", two ECUs answering, SEARCHING/BUS INIT noise
# loop 1: idle at the light
delay=1000
010D> 41 0D 00
010C> 41 0C 0C 80
0105> 41 05 7B
0110> 41 10 00 C8
015E> 41 5E 00 10

delay=1000

010C> 41 0C 0C 80

0110> 41 10 00 C8
015E> 41 5E 00 10
